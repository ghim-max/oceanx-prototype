# Meet Nada: a seagrass scientist (video)
Source: OceanX Media, "The Scientist Bringing Seagrass to Life",
youtube.com/watch?v=9XX4qY16V9Y (transcript, auto-captions cleaned)

I'm Nada, a Tunisian research scientist, and I'm the first resident with
OceanX Education.

My project was to build Seagrass Stories with immersive media. We built a
whole habitat with animals and plants, so people feel and understand what we
do to restore and to conserve the seagrass.

It was a good place to meet people from all over the world. Each visit here
to OceanXplorer, I connect with new people with different perspectives.

The young explorers come with curiosity. They are ready to learn. When they
ask questions, this also adds new things to think about. Meeting people from
different fields, like artists, scientists, and people from technology, XR
and VR, will change the whole story.

My PhD is the classic part of marine ecology. Now, with the residency and
with OceanX, it brings a whole new perspective, which is the technology. I
will use it to showcase what I do under the ocean. It will be more immersive
and more emotional. It is a new thing to mix science, technology and culture.
This will help me a lot in my PhD and my future research too.
