# Map the chain (Create activity)
Source: OceanX, seagrassstoriesforschools.oceanx.org (Educator Guide, Create stage)

In the Create stage (15 minutes), groups of 2 to 3 learners design an
experience that helps someone else understand an idea about ocean restoration.
The experience can be a game, a video, a comic or an installation.

Each group uses 6 to 10 Post-its. The Post-its should show a real choice or
action, what it causes, how the habitat responds, how things change over time,
and what the group wants someone to understand.

Groups then connect their Post-its with arrows to show how one change leads to
the next: what happens because of what. This builds a causal chain:
Choice or Action, then Consequence, then Habitat Response, then Change Over Time.

Prompts for groups: What choice or action happens? What happens because of it?
How does the habitat respond? How do things change over time?

The 6 to 10 Post-it limit gives enough elements to explore relationships
between parts of the system while keeping the system manageable.

Evidence of learning: learners communicate their understanding through a
causal chain that links a choice to its consequence, the habitat response and
change over time.

Learning outcome (Do): create an experience that uses choices, consequences
and change over time to communicate an idea about ocean restoration.

For university learners, the activity becomes a short research question: one
decision is traced through a seagrass ecosystem over time.
