# Seagrass Stories: Educator Guide stages
Source: OceanX, seagrassstoriesforschools.oceanx.org/educatorguide.html

Session: 4 stages x 15 minutes. Groups of 2 to 3 learners.
Facilitators focus on curiosity, questions, connections and evidence of learning.

## Explore: Enter the meadow (15 min)
Divide the three videos among groups. Learners hold two questions in mind
while watching, then debrief the full set using See, Think, Wonder.
See: What do you notice?
Think: What is happening? What relationships can you see?
Wonder: What questions does this raise? What do you want to understand more about?
Evidence of learning: learners notice details and relationships, offer
interpretations, and generate questions they can investigate.
Learners get time to notice before explaining. Facilitators capture
different interpretations and questions for later.
(See, Think, Wonder is adapted from Harvard Project Zero.)

## Investigate: Look behind the game (15 min)
Play Seagrass Stories as a scientist, examining change, timescale and recovery.
What changes quickly?
What might take longer in the ocean?
What evidence would tell you the meadow is recovering?
Evidence of learning: learners distinguish between immediate game responses
and ecological change, and identify possible evidence of recovery.

## Reflect: What did you discover? (15 min)
Learners reflect individually, then share and compare thinking with others.
They may return to earlier notes.
What changed how you think about ocean restoration?
What are you still wondering?
Evidence of learning: learners articulate a shift, connection or new
understanding, and identify remaining questions.

## Create: Bring ideas to life (15 min)
See create-activity.md.

## Materials
Internet-connected display and student devices where possible.
Access to the Seagrass Stories webpage and game.
One Learner Graphic Organiser per learner or group.
6 to 10 Post-its per group. Pens or markers.

## After the session, facilitators record
Where curiosity appeared. Which connections learners made. What the
creations revealed about understanding. Where the experience could be
strengthened.
