# OceanX Content Insights Loop - Product Context

## Product
Internal tool for OceanX Education (ocean science non-profit) that turns learner feedback into content decisions.

## Users
- **Primary:** Content managers deciding which educational content to reuse, adapt or drop for the next partner.
- **Secondary:** Learners completing content and giving feedback.

## Principles
1. Insights over charts - surface meaning, not raw data
2. Plain language - no jargon, no acronyms unexplained
3. One decision per screen - reduce cognitive load
4. Human review is the final step - AI recommendations require OceanX sign-off
5. Calm, cinematic, ocean-led aesthetic

## Content domain
Seagrass science: carbon storage, habitat loss, habitat restoration. Real research, fictional organisations and names.

## Screens
1. **Home** `/` - entry point, 3 pathways
2. **Content Library** `/library` - browse and filter components; see versions
3. **Learner View** `/learn/:versionId` - experience content; submit feedback
4. **Insights** `/insights` - metrics and AI-generated content recommendations
